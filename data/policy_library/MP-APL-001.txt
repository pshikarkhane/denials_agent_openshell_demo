APPEALS LEVELS AND DEADLINES
Policy ID: MP-APL-001
Payer: Meridian Community Health Plan
Payer ID: MERIDIAN_COMM
Effective date: 2026-03-01
Version history: Version 3.0, effective 2026-03-01. The requirements below carry forward unchanged from Version 2.0 (effective 2025-01-01) and apply to dates of service on or after 2025-01-01.

Section 1  Purpose and scope
This policy establishes the structured process by which providers may contest adverse claim determinations issued by Meridian Community Health Plan. It defines the available levels of appeal and the corresponding timeframes within which each request must be submitted.

Section 2.1
A provider may request a first-level appeal of an adverse claim determination. Such a request must be submitted within the appeal window specified in Section 7.

Section 2.2
A provider who disagrees with the first-level outcome may request a second-level review. This request must be submitted within the second-level window specified in Section 7.

Section 7  Key parameters and general provisions
7.1 Appeal deadline: 180 days from the date of the claim determination notice.
7.2 Timely filing limit: 180 days from the date of service.
7.3 Corrected claims: must be received within 365 days of the date of the notice.
7.4 Appeals address: Appeals Unit, P.O. Box 5100, Meridian City, ST 00001
7.5 Second-level review: must be requested within 180 days of the date of the first-level appeal decision notice.
