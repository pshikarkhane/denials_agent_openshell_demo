MEMBER COST SHARING AND DEDUCTIBLES
Policy ID: MP-BEN-002
Payer: Meridian Community Health Plan
Payer ID: MERIDIAN_COMM
Effective date: 2026-03-01
Version history: Version 3.0, effective 2026-03-01. The requirements below carry forward unchanged from Version 2.0 (effective 2025-01-01) and apply to dates of service on or after 2025-01-01.

Section 1  Purpose and scope
This policy establishes the principles governing how member financial responsibility is determined and applied under Meridian Community Health Plan. It describes the circumstances in which deductible amounts, cost sharing, and related member obligations are assessed in connection with covered services.

Section 2.1
When a member's deductible has already been satisfied prior to the date of service, no further deductible amount may be applied to the claim for that date of service. Meridian Community Health Plan will recognize the satisfied deductible and process the claim without assessing additional deductible responsibility to the member. The member's financial obligation for that service will be calculated as if the deductible has been fully met.

Section 2.2
Where Meridian Community Health Plan has applied the deductible in error, the plan will correct the claim and remove the erroneously applied deductible amount. Any member payment collected as a result of the erroneous deductible application will be adjusted accordingly. The claim will be reprocessed to reflect the member's correct cost sharing responsibility.

Section 7  Key parameters and general provisions
7.1 Appeal deadline: 180 days from the date of the claim determination notice.
7.2 Timely filing limit: 180 days from the date of service.
7.3 Corrected claims: must be received within 365 days of the date of the notice.
7.4 Appeals address: Appeals Unit, P.O. Box 5100, Meridian City, ST 00001
