MEMBER COST SHARING AND DEDUCTIBLES
Policy ID: MP-BEN-002
Payer: Meridian Community Health Plan
Payer ID: MERIDIAN_COMM
Effective date: 2026-03-01
Version history: Version 3.0, effective 2026-03-01. The requirements below carry forward unchanged from Version 2.0 (effective 2025-01-01) and apply to dates of service on or after 2025-01-01.

Section 1  Purpose and scope
This policy establishes the principles governing how Meridian Community Health Plan applies member cost sharing, including deductibles, to covered services. It is intended to ensure that financial responsibility is assigned accurately and consistently across all eligible members.

Section 5.1
When a member's deductible has already been satisfied before the date of service, the plan shall not apply any additional deductible amount to the claim for that service. Claims processing must verify the accumulated deductible status as of the date of service and adjudicate the claim accordingly. If a deductible was applied despite prior satisfaction, the plan shall correct the adjudication and reimburse the member or provider as appropriate.

Section 5.2
In circumstances where the plan applied a deductible in error, Meridian Community Health Plan shall reprocess the affected claim to remove the erroneous member responsibility. Any amount collected from the member as a result of the error must be refunded promptly once the error is identified and confirmed. The member and the servicing provider shall be notified of the corrected determination.

Section 7  Key parameters and general provisions
7.1 Appeal deadline: 180 days from the date of the claim determination notice.
7.2 Timely filing limit: 180 days from the date of service.
7.3 Corrected claims: must be received within 365 days of the date of the notice.
7.4 Appeals address: Appeals Unit, P.O. Box 5100, Meridian City, ST 00001
