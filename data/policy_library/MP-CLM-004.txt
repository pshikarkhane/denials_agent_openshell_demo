CLAIM SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS, DUPLICATE CLAIMS AND EDIT RULES
Policy ID: MP-CLM-004
Payer: Terravista Health
Payer ID: TERRAVISTA_HEALTH
Effective date: 2026-04-05
Version history: Version 3.0, effective 2026-04-05. The requirements below carry forward unchanged from Version 2.0 (effective 2025-01-01) and apply to dates of service on or after 2025-01-01.

Section 1  Purpose and scope
This policy establishes the standards Terravista Health applies to the submission, processing, and validation of claims to promote accurate and timely adjudication. It defines the expectations for complete claim data, the handling of duplicate submissions, and the application of edit rules across covered services.

Section 3.1
Each claim submitted to Terravista Health must contain all required data elements and any applicable modifiers at the time of submission. Claims lacking required data elements or necessary modifiers may be rejected or denied until a complete and corrected version is received. Providers remain responsible for ensuring that the claim as submitted is complete and accurate.

Section 3.4
When a claim is denied or returned for correction, the provider may file a corrected claim within the payer corrected-claim window established by Terravista Health. A corrected claim submitted after this window has closed may not be eligible for reprocessing. Providers should clearly identify a resubmission as a corrected claim to support proper handling.

Section 2.4
A denied claim must represent a distinct service and must not be a duplicate of a claim that has already been adjudicated by Terravista Health. Claims identified as duplicates of previously adjudicated services will not be considered for separate payment. Providers should confirm the status of prior submissions before resubmitting to avoid duplicate denials.

Section 4.2
The billed diagnosis must support the billed procedure in accordance with the applicable Terravista Health edit. Claims in which the reported diagnosis does not support the procedure under the edit may be denied. Providers are expected to report diagnoses that accurately reflect the clinical reason for the service rendered.

Section 4.3
The clinical note associated with the service must document a diagnosis that supports the billed procedure. Documentation that fails to establish a supporting diagnosis may result in denial or recovery of payment upon review. Providers must maintain records sufficient to demonstrate that the procedure was warranted by the documented condition.

Section 7  Key parameters and general provisions
7.1 Appeal deadline: 90 days from the date of the claim determination notice.
7.2 Timely filing limit: 90 days from the date of service.
7.3 Corrected claims: must be received within 180 days of the date of the notice.
7.4 Appeals address: Appeals Unit, P.O. Box 3300, Terravista, ST 00003
