CLAIM SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS, DUPLICATE CLAIMS AND EDIT RULES
Policy ID: MP-CLM-004
Payer: Terravista Health
Payer ID: TERRAVISTA_HEALTH
Effective date: 2026-04-05
Version history: Version 3.0, effective 2026-04-05. The requirements below carry forward unchanged from Version 2.0 (effective 2025-01-01) and apply to dates of service on or after 2025-01-01.

Section 1  Purpose and scope
This policy establishes the standards Terravista Health applies when evaluating claim submissions, duplicate claims, and automated edit rules. It is intended to promote accurate claim processing and consistent adjudication across all applicable provider submissions.

Section 2.1
A claim must contain all required data elements and modifiers at the time it is submitted. Terravista Health will evaluate each claim to confirm that these elements and modifiers were present as submitted. Claims that do not meet this requirement may be subject to further review under this policy.

Section 2.2
A corrected claim may be filed provided it is submitted within the payer corrected-claim window. Terravista Health requires that any correction to a previously submitted claim fall within this window to be considered. Corrected claims meeting this requirement will be processed in accordance with this policy.

Section 2.3
A denied claim must not be a duplicate of a claim that has already been adjudicated. Terravista Health will verify that the claim under review does not match an already-adjudicated claim before further handling. Claims identified as duplicates of adjudicated claims will be addressed under the duplicate provisions of this policy.

Section 2.4
The diagnosis billed on a claim must support the billed procedure according to the applicable payer edit. Terravista Health will apply its edit rules to confirm that the billed diagnosis aligns with the billed procedure. Claims that fail this edit may be subject to additional review under this policy.

Section 2.5
The clinical note must document a diagnosis that supports the procedure performed. Terravista Health requires that supporting documentation in the clinical record reflect a diagnosis consistent with the billed procedure. Claims lacking such documentation may be reviewed in accordance with this policy.

Section 3  Corrections and resubmission
An appeal does not cure missing or invalid claim data. When a claim is denied for missing or invalid required information, the provider should submit a corrected claim. The corrected-claim deadline is stated in Section 7.3.

Section 7  Key parameters and general provisions
7.1 Appeal deadline: 90 days from the date of the claim determination notice.
7.2 Timely filing limit: 90 days from the date of service.
7.3 Corrected claims: must be received within 180 days of the date of the notice.
7.4 Appeals address: Appeals Unit, P.O. Box 3300, Terravista, ST 00003
