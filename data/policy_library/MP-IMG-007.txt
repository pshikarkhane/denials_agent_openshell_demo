ADVANCED IMAGING: LUMBAR SPINE MRI MEDICAL NECESSITY
Policy ID: MP-IMG-007
Payer: Meridian Community Health Plan
Payer ID: MERIDIAN_COMM
Effective date: 2026-04-15
Version history: Version 3.0, effective 2026-04-15. The requirements below carry forward unchanged from Version 2.0 (effective 2025-01-01) and apply to dates of service on or after 2025-01-01.

Section 1  Purpose and scope
This policy establishes the clinical criteria under which Meridian Community Health Plan considers magnetic resonance imaging of the lumbar spine to be medically necessary. It applies to requests for advanced lumbar spine imaging submitted for coverage review under the plan's utilization management program.

Section 2.1(a)
The member must have completed at least 6 weeks of conservative therapy prior to the request for lumbar spine MRI. Documentation in the medical record must substantiate that this period of conservative treatment was undertaken.

Section 2.1(b)
A neurological deficit or a recognized red flag finding must be documented in the clinical record supporting the request. This documentation must be present to establish medical necessity for the imaging study.

Section 2.1(c)
There must be no prior imaging of the same lumbar spine region performed within the preceding 12 months. The absence of such imaging within this 12-month timeframe must be confirmed before the request is approved.
