ADVANCED IMAGING: LUMBAR SPINE CT MEDICAL NECESSITY
Policy ID: MP-IMG-012
Payer: Meridian Community Health Plan
Payer ID: MERIDIAN_COMM
Effective date: 2026-02-01
Version history: Version 1.0, current.

Section 1  Purpose and scope
This policy establishes the criteria under which Meridian Community Health Plan considers computed tomography of the lumbar spine to be medically necessary. It is intended to guide coverage determinations for advanced imaging of the lumbar region and to support consistent, clinically appropriate review.

Section 2.1(a)
The member must have completed at least 4 weeks of conservative therapy prior to the requested imaging. Documentation of this conservative treatment period is required for the request to be considered medically necessary.

Section 2.1(b)
A neurological deficit or a documented red flag finding must be present and recorded in the clinical record. This documentation must support the need for lumbar spine CT imaging.

Section 2.1(c)
There must be no prior imaging of the same lumbar region performed within the preceding 6 months. The absence of such recent imaging of the same region is required for coverage under this policy.
