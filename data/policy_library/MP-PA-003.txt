PRIOR AUTHORIZATION AND RETROACTIVE AUTHORIZATION
Policy ID: MP-PA-003
Payer: Halcyon Health Plan
Payer ID: HALCYON_PLAN
Effective date: 2026-04-01
Version history: Version 3.0, effective 2026-04-01. The requirements below carry forward unchanged from Version 2.0 (effective 2025-01-01) and apply to dates of service on or after 2025-01-01.

Section 1  Purpose and scope
This policy establishes the standards under which Halcyon Health Plan evaluates requests for prior authorization and retroactive authorization of covered services. It applies to participating and non-participating providers who seek approval for services that are subject to authorization requirements under the member's benefit plan.

Section 2.1
Certain services identified by Halcyon Health Plan require prior authorization before they are rendered to the member. Providers must obtain approval from the plan in advance of delivering any service designated as requiring prior authorization, and failure to secure such approval may result in denial of the associated claim. The requesting provider is responsible for confirming whether a given service is subject to this requirement prior to furnishing care.

Section 4.1
A request for retroactive authorization may be considered when the service was urgent or emergent in nature and the member's clinical condition made it impractical to obtain authorization before care was delivered. The provider must demonstrate that the circumstances required immediate treatment and that seeking advance approval would have delayed necessary care or placed the member at risk. Supporting clinical documentation must accompany the request to substantiate the urgent or emergent nature of the service.

Section 4.2
A retroactive authorization request must be submitted to Halcyon Health Plan within 14 days of the date of service. Requests received after this timeframe may be denied for failure to meet the submission window, absent a demonstrated extenuating circumstance acceptable to the plan. The provider bears responsibility for ensuring that the request and all required documentation are received by the plan within the applicable period.

Section 7  Key parameters and general provisions
7.1 Appeal deadline: 120 days from the date of the claim determination notice.
7.2 Timely filing limit: 365 days from the date of service.
7.3 Corrected claims: must be received within 180 days of the date of the notice.
7.4 Appeals address: Appeals Unit, P.O. Box 7200, Halcyon Bay, ST 00002
7.5 Retroactive authorization window: 14 days from the date of service.
