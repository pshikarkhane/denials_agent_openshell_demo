PRIOR AUTHORIZATION AND RETROACTIVE AUTHORIZATION
Policy ID: MP-PA-003
Payer: Halcyon Health Plan
Payer ID: HALCYON_PLAN
Effective date: 2026-04-01
Version history: Version 3.0, effective 2026-04-01. The requirements below carry forward unchanged from Version 2.0 (effective 2025-01-01) and apply to dates of service on or after 2025-01-01.

Section 1  Purpose and scope
This policy establishes the framework under which Halcyon Health Plan evaluates requests for prior authorization and retroactive authorization of covered services. It applies to participating and non-participating providers who seek approval for services rendered to plan members.

Section 2.1
The service under review requires prior authorization from Halcyon Health Plan before it is furnished to the member. Providers must obtain this authorization in accordance with the plan's established processes. Services identified as requiring prior authorization are subject to the review provisions set forth in this policy.

Section 2.2
Retroactive authorization may be considered when the service was urgent or emergent in nature, such that obtaining authorization beforehand was not feasible. The provider must demonstrate that the urgent or emergent circumstances prevented authorization from being secured prior to the service being rendered.

Section 2.3
A request for retroactive authorization must be submitted within 14 days of the date of service. Requests meeting this 14-day submission window will be accepted for review under this policy.

Section 7  Key parameters and general provisions
7.1 Appeal deadline: 120 days from the date of the claim determination notice.
7.2 Timely filing limit: 365 days from the date of service.
7.3 Corrected claims: must be received within 180 days of the date of the notice.
7.4 Appeals address: Appeals Unit, P.O. Box 7200, Halcyon Bay, ST 00002
7.5 Retroactive authorization window: 14 days from the date of service.
