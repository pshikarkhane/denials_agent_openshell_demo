PRIOR AUTHORIZATION AND RETROACTIVE AUTHORIZATION
Policy ID: MP-PA-030
Payer: Meridian Community Health Plan
Payer ID: MERIDIAN_COMM
Effective date: 2026-04-01
Version history: Version 1.0, current.

Section 1  Purpose and scope
This policy establishes the framework under which Meridian Community Health Plan reviews requests for prior authorization and retroactive authorization of covered services. It sets out the circumstances in which authorization must be obtained and the conditions under which a retroactive request may be considered.

Section 2.1
The service under review requires prior authorization from Meridian Community Health Plan before it is rendered. The requesting provider must secure this authorization as a condition of coverage for the service.

Section 2.2
Retroactive authorization may be considered when the service was urgent or emergent in nature, such that prior authorization could not be obtained before the service was delivered. The clinical circumstances must demonstrate that the urgent or emergent condition prevented the provider from seeking authorization in advance.

Section 2.3
The request for retroactive authorization must be submitted within 7 days of the date of service. A request that is not submitted within 7 days of the date of service does not meet this requirement.

Section 7  Key parameters and general provisions
7.1 Appeal deadline: 180 days from the date of the claim determination notice.
7.2 Timely filing limit: 180 days from the date of service.
7.3 Corrected claims: must be received within 365 days of the date of the notice.
7.4 Appeals address: Appeals Unit, P.O. Box 5100, Meridian City, ST 00001
7.5 Retroactive authorization window: 7 days from the date of service.
