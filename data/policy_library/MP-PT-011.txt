OUTPATIENT THERAPY VISIT LIMITS
Policy ID: MP-PT-011
Payer: Halcyon Health Plan
Payer ID: HALCYON_PLAN
Effective date: 2026-04-20
Version history: Version 3.0, effective 2026-04-20. The requirements below carry forward unchanged from Version 2.0 (effective 2025-01-01) and apply to dates of service on or after 2025-01-01.

Section 1  Purpose and scope
This policy establishes the clinical standards under which Halcyon Health Plan evaluates coverage for outpatient physical, occupational, and speech therapy services. It is intended to promote consistent review of therapy care and to clarify the documentation expected to support continued treatment.

Section 2.1
Coverage requires that the medical record demonstrate measurable functional improvement resulting from the therapy provided. The documentation must show objective changes in the member's functional status that are attributable to the course of treatment.

Section 2.2
The treating physician must have recertified the plan of care for the member. This recertification must be reflected in the clinical record as support for continued therapy services.

Section 2.3
This provision applies when the denied visit falls within visits 21 through 30 of the calendar year. The requirements of this policy are evaluated with respect to visits occurring in that range. Visits 21 through 30 may be considered when functional improvement is documented and the plan of care is recertified. Visits beyond 30 are not covered.

Section 7  Key parameters and general provisions
7.1 Appeal deadline: 120 days from the date of the claim determination notice.
7.2 Timely filing limit: 365 days from the date of service.
7.3 Corrected claims: must be received within 180 days of the date of the notice.
7.4 Appeals address: Appeals Unit, P.O. Box 7200, Halcyon Bay, ST 00002
7.5 Outpatient therapy visit limit: 20 visits per calendar year.
7.6 Maximum visits with documented functional improvement and a recertified plan of care: 30 visits per calendar year.
