OUTPATIENT THERAPY VISIT LIMITS
Policy ID: MP-PT-011
Payer: Halcyon Health Plan
Payer ID: HALCYON_PLAN
Effective date: 2026-04-20
Version history: Version 3.0, effective 2026-04-20. The requirements below carry forward unchanged from Version 2.0 (effective 2025-01-01) and apply to dates of service on or after 2025-01-01.

Section 1  Purpose and scope
This policy describes the conditions under which Halcyon Health Plan provides coverage for outpatient physical, occupational, and speech therapy services furnished to eligible members. It establishes the clinical documentation standards and review criteria used to determine whether continued therapy visits are considered medically necessary and eligible for reimbursement.

Section 4.2
Coverage of continued outpatient therapy requires that the clinical record document measurable functional improvement attributable to the services provided. The treating therapist must describe objective and comparable findings that demonstrate meaningful progress toward the established treatment goals. In the absence of documented measurable functional improvement, ongoing therapy may be determined to be maintenance in nature and not eligible for coverage.

Section 4.3
Continued coverage requires that the supervising physician have recertified the plan of care at the intervals specified by the plan. The recertification must confirm that the ongoing therapy remains appropriate to the member's condition and consistent with the stated treatment objectives. Documentation supporting the physician recertification must be retained in the member's clinical record and made available upon request.

Section 4.4
Coverage of additional therapy under an approved exception is available only when the visit count remains within the exception cap of 30. Requests that would cause the member's cumulative covered visits to exceed the exception cap of 30 will not be eligible for coverage under this provision. The treating provider must monitor the running visit count to ensure services are furnished within this limit.

Section 7  Key parameters and general provisions
7.1 Appeal deadline: 120 days from the date of the claim determination notice.
7.2 Timely filing limit: 365 days from the date of service.
7.3 Corrected claims: must be received within 180 days of the date of the notice.
7.4 Appeals address: Appeals Unit, P.O. Box 7200, Halcyon Bay, ST 00002
7.5 Outpatient therapy visit limit: 20 visits per calendar year.
7.6 Visit cap with documented exception: 30 visits per calendar year.
