TIMELY FILING OF CLAIMS
Policy ID: MP-TF-001
Payer: Terravista Health
Payer ID: TERRAVISTA_HEALTH
Effective date: 2026-03-15
Version history: Version 3.0, effective 2026-03-15. The requirements below carry forward unchanged from Version 2.0 (effective 2025-01-01) and apply to dates of service on or after 2025-01-01.

Section 1  Purpose and scope
This policy establishes the standards governing the timely submission of claims to Terravista Health for reimbursement consideration. It applies to all participating and non-participating providers who render covered services to members enrolled under plans administered by Terravista Health.

Section 2.1
Claims must be submitted to Terravista Health within 90 days of the date of service. Submissions received after this filing period may be denied for failure to meet the timely filing requirement.

Section 2.2
Providers must retain proof of clearinghouse acceptance demonstrating that the claim was accepted within the applicable filing limit. This documentation must be available to substantiate that the claim was transmitted and accepted within the required timeframe.

Section 7  Key parameters and general provisions
7.1 Appeal deadline: 90 days from the date of the claim determination notice.
7.2 Timely filing limit: 90 days from the date of service.
7.3 Corrected claims: must be received within 180 days of the date of the notice.
7.4 Appeals address: Appeals Unit, P.O. Box 3300, Terravista, ST 00003
