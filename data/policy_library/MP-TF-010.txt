TIMELY FILING OF CLAIMS
Policy ID: MP-TF-010
Payer: Terravista Health
Payer ID: TERRAVISTA_HEALTH
Effective date: 2024-01-01
Status: SUPERSEDED. Replaced by MP-TF-001 (current version effective 2026-03-15). Version 1.0, effective 2024-01-01; this version last applied to dates of service on or before 2024-12-31.

Section 1  Purpose and scope
This policy establishes the standards for timely submission of claims to Terravista Health for consideration of reimbursement. It applies to all participating and non-participating providers who render covered services to eligible members.

Section 2.1
Providers must submit claims to Terravista Health within 60 days of the date of service. Claims received after this filing period will be considered untimely and may be denied for failure to meet the submission requirement.

Section 2.2
Providers must retain and be able to furnish proof of clearinghouse acceptance demonstrating that the claim was transmitted within the applicable filing limit. This documentation serves as evidence that the submission requirement was met and must be available upon request by Terravista Health.

Section 7  Key parameters and general provisions
7.1 Appeal deadline: 90 days from the date of the claim determination notice.
7.2 Timely filing limit: 60 days from the date of service.
7.3 Corrected claims: must be received within 180 days of the date of the notice.
7.4 Appeals address: Appeals Unit, P.O. Box 3300, Terravista, ST 00003
