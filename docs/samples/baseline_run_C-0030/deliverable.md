Reasoning
- The denial letter states the claim was received by the payer on 2026-07-15, after the 90-day filing limit, and that the filing deadline for the 2026-04-01 date of service was 2026-06-30 (source: denial_letter.txt).
- The claim history shows the claim was submitted to the clearinghouse on 2026-07-14, accepted by the clearinghouse on 2026-07-14, and received by the payer on 2026-07-15 (source: claim_history.txt).
- The 835 remittance advice confirms the date the claim was received by the payer was 2026-07-15 (source: eob_835.txt).
- MP-TF-001 Section 2.1 requires claims to be submitted within 90 days of the date of service; this was not met because submission/acceptance (2026-07-14) and payer receipt (2026-07-15) are both after the 2026-06-30 deadline (source: claim_history.txt; denial_letter.txt).
- MP-TF-001 Section 2.2 requires proof of clearinghouse acceptance demonstrating acceptance within the applicable filing limit; the available proof shows acceptance on 2026-07-14, after the 2026-06-30 deadline, so it does not establish timely filing (source: claim_history.txt).
- MP-TF-001 Section 7.2 sets the timely filing limit at 90 days from the date of service; the claim was not filed within that limit (source: denial_letter.txt).

No document in the record shows the claim was submitted or accepted on or before 2026-06-30. The office visit note for 2026-04-01 documents that the service occurred (source: notes/2026-04-01_visit.txt) but does not bear on timely filing.

Because the denial is for untimely filing and the record shows the claim was received after the deadline, the denial is supported by the governing policy and the record. There is no missing or invalid claim data element to correct (the service, CPT code 99213, diagnosis I10, and place of service 11 are all recorded on the claim; source: eob_835.txt), and a corrected claim would not change the filing date. MP-CLM-004 Section 3 limits corrected claims to missing or invalid required information (source: policy_library/MP-CLM-004.txt). Accordingly, an appeal is not supported, and a corrected claim would not change the outcome.
